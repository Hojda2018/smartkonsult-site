Cookie Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

1. In short

This website does not use cookies and does not store anything in your browser. That is why we don't show you a cookie banner.

2. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website can store in your browser, for example to remember a choice or to track you across websites. Similar technologies include localStorage and tracking pixels.

3. What we actually use

  • We do not use advertising, social media or analytics cookies.
  • We have not installed Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or any other tracking tools.
  • You choose the language through the page address, so it does not need to be stored in the browser.

4. External services

To run the page we use a few external providers. They receive your IP address, as with any request on the internet, but they do not set cookies through our website:

  • Netlify: hosts the website and receives form data, including your CV if you choose to send it.
  • Unsplash: some of the images on the website.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): only if you tap a WhatsApp button. The conversation opens in the WhatsApp app, which has its own policy.

5. If anything changes

If we ever use cookies that are not strictly necessary, we will ask for your consent first and update this page.

6. Contact

For any question, write to us at office@smartkonsultplus.ro. See also our Privacy Policy.
