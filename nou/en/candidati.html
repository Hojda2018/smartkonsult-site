Frequently asked questions

What candidates ask us

Do I have to pay anything?

No. We do not charge candidates for selection or for the job.

Do I need to speak German?

Not for every job. For each job we tell you whether German is needed and at what level.

Can I start without experience?

Yes, in logistics. We train you on warehouse processes before you start. For welding and construction you need experience in the trade.

Who pays for housing?

It depends on the contract. With posting, SmartKonsult pays; with a German contract, rent is deducted from your wages. We tell you in advance, for every job.

What documents do I need?

A valid ID card or passport. If you have certificates (welding, forklift), bring them. We prepare the rest of the paperwork together.
