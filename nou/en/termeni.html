2. Our services

SmartKonsult Plus SRL provides companies in Germany and Luxembourg with staffing consultancy, selection, project management and logistics training. For candidates, we look for suitable jobs, explain the conditions and help with paperwork, transport, housing and the first days at work.

3. Our services are free for candidates

We do not charge candidates any commission, file fee, placement fee or deposit. Our services are paid for by the companies we work with.

If anyone asks you for money on behalf of SmartKonsult Plus, do not pay and let us know immediately at office@smartkonsultplus.ro.

4. The employment contract

Depending on the job, there are two options, which we explain before you leave:

  • German contract: the employment contract is concluded directly with the German partner company, under German law.
  • Posting: the employment contract is concluded with SmartKonsult Plus SRL, under Romanian law, and the work takes place in Germany with the A1 certificate.

5. What we guarantee and what we don't

  • We only present real jobs from companies we have a contractual relationship with.
  • We present pay, working hours and conditions as they are set for the job at the time of publication.
  • We do not guarantee employment. The decision depends on the interview, the document check and the job requirements.
  • The amounts in the pay calculator are estimates for German tax class I, without children, church tax or bonuses. Actual income depends on your situation and the hours worked. The estimate is not tax advice.
