Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This policy explains how SmartKonsult Plus SRL collects, uses and protects the personal data of people who contact us, companies that request a quote and candidates who apply for a job. We comply with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Romanian Law No. 190/2018.

1. Who we are

SmartKonsult Plus SRL, Cluj-Napoca, Romania
Email: office@smartkonsultplus.ro
Phone: +40 774 772 117

2. What data we collect

  • From candidates: full name, phone (including WhatsApp), email, preferred sector, experience, level of German, your message and your CV, if you choose to send it.
  • From companies: company name, contact person, email, phone and the details of the request.
  • Documents you send us voluntarily by form, email or WhatsApp.
  • Minimal technical data: like any web server, the hosting provider logs the IP address and the page requested, for the operation and security of the website.

We do not use traffic analytics and do not track your behaviour on the website. We do not ask for sensitive data such as health or religious and political beliefs. If such data appears in a CV, we delete it.

3. Why we process data and on what legal basis

PurposeLegal basis
Assessing an application and presenting it to partner companiesPre-contractual steps at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
Replying to a company's request and preparing a quotePre-contractual steps (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
Contacting you by phone, WhatsApp or emailYour consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR)

4. Who we share data with

  • Partner companies in Germany and Luxembourg, only as far as needed for a job or a project.
  • Our IT service providers, who process data on our behalf: Netlify, for hosting the website and receiving the forms, including CVs, and our email provider. These providers may also process data outside the European Economic Area, on the basis of the standard contractual clauses provided for by the GDPR.
  • Public authorities, when the law requires us to.

We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

5. Third-party content

Some images on the website are loaded from Unsplash. When they load, your browser sends your IP address to this provider. The WhatsApp buttons open the WhatsApp app, which belongs to Meta and has its own privacy policy.

6. How long we keep data

  • Applications without employment: at most 2 years after the last contact.
  • Applications with employment: for the duration of the cooperation and afterwards in line with the statutory retention periods.
  • Company requests: at most 2 years after the last contact, if no cooperation begins.
  • WhatsApp messages and emails: at most 2 years, unless you ask for earlier deletion.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. You can withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing carried out before that.

To exercise any of these rights, write to us at office@smartkonsultplus.ro. We reply free of charge within 30 days at most.

You can lodge a complaint with the Romanian data protection authority (ANSPDCP), B-dul G-ral Gheorghe Magheru nr. 28-30, Bucharest, www.dataprotection.ro, or with the data protection authority of the country where you live.

8. Security

The website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Only the people in the company who handle recruitment and projects have access to CVs and contact details.

9. Minors

Our services are intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data about minors.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The version in force is the one published on this page, with the date of the last update shown above.
