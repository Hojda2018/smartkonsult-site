Frequently asked questions

What companies ask us

Who employs the workers?

It depends on the model. With a German contract, our German partner is the employer. With posting, the workers are employed by SmartKonsult Plus SRL on a Romanian contract and work at your site on the basis of the A1 certificate.

Who takes care of housing and transport?

We organise both. With a German contract, rent is deducted from wages. With posting, SmartKonsult pays for the housing. We put these details in writing before the start.

Can the workers be trained on our processes?

Yes. We train on warehouse processes, from goods receipt and picking to goods issue, and forklift training is delivered by our in-house instructor under DGUV Grundsatz 308-001.

Which countries do you work in?

Germany and Luxembourg.

What language do we communicate in?

German, Romanian or English. You have one point of contact for the whole project.
